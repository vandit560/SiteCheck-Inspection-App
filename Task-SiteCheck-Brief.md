# Task - SiteCheck Inspection App

## Overview

SiteCheck is an upcoming mobile app for building safety inspectors. Inspectors visit construction sites, go through a safety checklist and record what they find. Many sites are basements or outskirts with weak or no network, and inspectors cannot wait for a signal to do their job.

Build the inspector's mobile app. The backend isn't ready yet, so use the attached `mockApi.ts` as it is.

## Functional requirements

1. Inspectors see the inspections assigned to them and complete each one by going through the site's checklist.
2. Each checklist item is marked Pass, Fail or Not applicable, with a note for anything that fails.
3. Inspectors can do all of this without a network connection, and nothing they record is lost.
4. Completed inspections reach the office once the network is available.
